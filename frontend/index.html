What is a Digital Immune System (DIS)?

Academic Concept

A Digital Immune System combines observability, threat detection, automated protection, chaos testing, and self-recovery engineering to make software resilient against cyberattacks and operational failures.

1. Detect

Continuously observe application logs, login patterns, API traffic, and health metrics.

2. Defend

Analyze suspicious deviations, evaluate risk, and isolate malicious sources safely.

3. Recover & Evolve

Restore degraded services automatically and learn from historical incident patterns.

Six Core Components (Gartner DIS Framework)

Software Engineering
1. Observability

Captures real-time telemetry, latencies, errors, and authentication trails.

2. AI-Augmented Testing

Identifies weaknesses and edge cases before failures reach users.

3. Chaos Engineering

Injects controlled faults to verify resilience under stress.

4. Autoremediation

Executes automated containment and service recovery actions.

5. Site Reliability Engineering

Maintains availability targets, audit trails, and operational reliability.

6. Supply Chain Security

Protects codebases, packages, and deployment pipelines from tampering.
